# Test101 / V0.116 — live Rumble stamina recovery active gate

This checkpoint wires the first byte-level exclusion of the recovered ROM `$8ECC` natural-stamina routine into the live native Rumble loop.

At `$8EE0` the original 68000 executes `BTST #7,+$00(A0)` and skips recovery when the actor is not active. Native `active_mask` is the authoritative live/eliminated ownership bit, so eliminated Rumble actors now clear their recovery accumulator and cannot regain stamina after elimination.

The already recovered raw `$1D/$61` exclusions (`0B`, `0C`, linked `FF -> 0C`, and `05 + pose 4A`) remain exact as a raw predicate. Native states without a demonstrated raw mapping continue to pass zero instead of fabricating a mapping.

Host proof: `tools/test_rumble_recovery_active_gate.c` drives a fighter at recovery tick 127 first with its active bit cleared (no recovery, accumulator reset) and then active (exact +1 at 128 ticks).
