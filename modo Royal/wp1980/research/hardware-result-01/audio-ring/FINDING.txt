WP1980 isolated PCM ring-tail correction

Status
The actual FAST_68000 fill path failed a host regression before the change
and passes after a minimal ring-tail bound. The new SCSP.ELF builds and is
accepted by the production loader. This is a separately demonstrated audio
corruption defect; its occurrence in the reported physical run, its effect on
that run's sound, and its connection to the result-screen fault are unproven.

Defect and change
The fast decoder writes consecutive words. Refill targets are aligned to 32
samples, but work may request 64. At ring index 4064, a 64-sample decode crossed
the 4096-sample ring end. In the DSP layout it left 32 stale samples in its own
ring, replaced the interpolation guard, and overwrote 31 samples of the next
voice. The final voice could instead overwrite following sound-RAM metadata.

The valid regression witness uses played=4128, write_at=write_end=8160, then
353 elapsed SCSP frames. Production advance reaches played=4192 and
write_end=8224 without a stall or underrun. Both pre-existing checks permit
the bad write. This witness establishes the code defect, not physical timing.

Only work/hwfix01/audio/native/wf_scsp_sfx.c was edited in production.
Fast fill now caps count at 4096-(write_at&4095). The next bounded work call
continues at index zero, preserving decoded order and the existing guard
refresh. The old comment claiming alignment alone prevents crossing was
corrected. The >512-frame stall policy, deadlines, command logic, DSP program,
assembly decoder and music-disabled configuration were not changed.

Regression evidence
run_regression.py compiles the actual production source with FAST_68000.
Only the sound-RAM base is relocated and the 68000 assembly boundary replaced
by a deterministic contiguous decoder. The 14-byte decoder ABI is retained.
The test checks all four voices, both supported strides, ring sample order,
other-voice and surrounding-memory preservation, guard mirroring, ordinary
refill, available-tail limiting, exact ring end, initial-fill completion and
empty work. It does not establish ADPCM waveform fidelity or physical timing.

RED:   red.json   -- compiled cleanly; 13 cases, 20 failed assertions, exit 1.
GREEN: green.json -- compiled cleanly; 13 cases, 0 failed assertions, exit 0.
The same test source was used for both. Eight crossing cases failed before;
the five non-crossing controls passed. Boundary refills now complete in two
bounded calls, with no stale, neighboring or guard samples.
