Nonblocking follow-up (custom builds only):
native/src/wf_r18_present.c accepts any header size >=4096 divisible by2048.
With an explicit non-shipped value such as6144, a legacy R18H/R18D count fitting
6144 bytes can round header_bytes up to8192, exceeding hot_header. Restrict the
macro to supported4096/8192/16384 values or additionally reject
header_bytes > sizeof hot_header before the second read. Current4096 and
default16384 builds cannot take this overflow path.
