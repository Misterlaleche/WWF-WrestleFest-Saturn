ISOLATED PCM DEADLINE CORRECTION — design/plan before implementation
2026-10-29. Authorized scope: validation only, no SH changes or real-disc media.

Measured fault, original SCSP b882... and unmodified M68K/DSP stepper:
cmd02,3 ongoing direct effects+PCM3, future ring publication512Fs early.
The worst first poll is660Fs late after300080 CPUcycles from publication.
230324 cycles(76.75%) in that interval are ADPCM block decode, not Python
clock granularity. The1-instruction run has maximum100cycles per execute;
the256cycle run gives658Fs rather than660Fs. A64sample block costs12830
cycles=50.12Fs;256sample prefill49886cycles=194.87Fs/4.42ms. One work() can
do4x64samples, approximately200Fs/4.55ms, before returning to deadlines.
Do not add inclusive work() time to its decode() children.

Current timing contract:
B028 adds one per event due>256Fs and another due>2205Fs. Both branches
execute known kinds; this is not a drop count. kind4 only changes mailbox
FM-active metadata; kind1/2 change musical OKI/bank. FM hardware writes and
FM loss belong to the SH worker/B03A. B028 and PCM underruns remain blocked
until evidence permits a new, explicit gate; this experiment never hides them.

Approaches considered:
1. Only shrink ordinary refill. Smallest change, but still decodes256samples
   after the requested start and cannot by itself fix causal launch latency.
2. Prepare one upcoming musical launch before its timestamp, and bound
   ordinary refill work. Chosen first isolated experiment. It attacks the
   measured cause without larger ring, earlier SH publication or clock change.
3. Stage every future SFX sequence launch as well. Broader engine work; reserve
   this for evidence that simultaneous scheduled SFX starts still break the
   deadline. No speculative4-voice asynchronous command queue in experiment1.

Minimum contracts by scenario:
A. PCM3 idle, future start already in existing ring: decode its initial256
   samples ahead of due into inactive PCM3 storage. No gain/keyon before when.
B. PCM3 still playing: use a512B staging buffer plus decoder/identity metadata
   (estimate<560B total) so preparation never overwrites audible ring data.
   At due, preserve original busy/suppress/stop semantics. A start while busy
   is still ignored; preparation must not force a restart. A prior scheduled
   stop permits commit. If needed, copy256samples after stop and enable only
   when due. Measure copy/control time, do not assume exact zero latency.
C. Bank/sample-select bytes: preparation simulates only the ordered lookahead
   in a local descriptor; real music_bank/oki_pending/consumer still commit at
   their timestamps. Preserve generation and raw event order; invalidate on
   generation, changed head identity, STOP or a mismatching prepared sample.
D. Ordinary refill: total globally bounded chunk, initially32decoded samples
   per service call (about26Fs), round-robin across four voices. Preserve every
   decoded value/ring safety margin and sample end. This adds no lookahead or
   queue capacity. Confirm producer bandwidth and zero real32bit underruns.
E. Mail/SFX launch: measure request->ACK and request->first new PCM output,
   including the prelaunch refill. Reduce its ordinary refill to the same
   bounded service contract; don't redefine ACK to precede accepted launch.
   Preserve all98commands, priorities, deterministic preemption, inherited bank,
   suppression, stop, delayed event order, and generation separation of PCM3.
F. Simultaneous scheduled SFX launches remain an explicit risk: three fresh
   synchronous256sample prefills cost~585Fs even without other work. If the
   measured corpus exercises this, experiment1 cannot claim full delivery;
   add future per-voice staging only with its own red/green proof and sizes.
