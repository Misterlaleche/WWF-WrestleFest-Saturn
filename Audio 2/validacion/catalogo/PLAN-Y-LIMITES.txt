Complete18-music +98-effects integration evidence plan — 2026-10-29

This is a requirement/source audit, not a passing execution report. No new
audio algorithm,canonical firmware,original ROM,R04 or disc media is changed.

Actual command ownership:
00 goes through BOTH worker FIFO64 and mailbox FIFO64, ordered stop.
01..12 and1F go only to the music worker when profile4D46 is present.
20..81 go through wf_native_queue,ARCADE_WORD7 and the68000 SFX command engine.
20 is the original empty command.13..1E are not among the18 scores and must
be counted as UNIMPLEMENTED, not mistaken for successful catalogue coverage.
The low byte selects the command; raw16 is retained in mailbox diagnostics.

Three effect sequences own PCM0..2. Musical OKI owns PCM3;32YM operators use
the32SCSP slots and PCM uses the DSP backend. A generation change stops FM
and musicalPCM3, but preserves effects0..2. A delayed old mailbox stop must
not silence a newer music generation. Fade1F must not stop unrelated effects.
The3-effect priority oracle is the recovered A15/A16 reference; equal priority
is allowed to preempt,lower priority can be legitimately rejected. Selector2
retains the previous bank. PCM tail can outlast logical sequence activity.

Fade completion:
Native0620 decrements the fade counter every two timerB handlers; on completion
it clearsC062,C006/C007 and enters03A4 (key-off all FM plus channel8 stop).
Observe those states and R01 final YM/OKI events,FM keymask/levels,queued-event
drain and absence of renewed music. Eight seconds without error is insufficient.
Effects tails may remain audible; global RMS need not become zero while SFX play.
Do not treat intentional musical rests or natural one-shot end as missing audio.

Minimum live metrics:
Native/music profile,worker/firmware RUNNING,generation/ACK,full HWRAM code
identity,score cycles and commands,FIFO occupancies/head age,FMdrop16/late counts,
mail ACK/results,host queue submitted/completed/unimplemented/priority/overflow,

Positive delivery requires zero real/mailbox PCMunderruns,FMdrops,unknown
required commands,queue loss,ignored/disabled audio,transport/music faults or
graphics regression. Priority rejection is allowed only where oracle predicts it.
Metadata and counters absent -> blocked,not zero. No tolerance/lead changes.

Containment distinction confirmed in source:
wf_scsp_sfx.c intentionally stops an exhausted voice and increments underruns
without corrupting firmware/game. The old test_sfx.c assertion requiringfault
is obsolete; update only that test to require nofault,underrun1,affected voice
stopped and another voice alive. Positive delivery still fails for any underrun.
No recommendation is made to restore a fatal engine policy.
