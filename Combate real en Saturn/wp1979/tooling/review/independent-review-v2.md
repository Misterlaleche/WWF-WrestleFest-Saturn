The parent is investigating further performance and manual-route tile coverage. The older candidate-1 average is not final evidence.

**M2 — Static placement margin must not be called measured free master stack.** The candidate-1 layout verifier reports 105,472 bytes from .bss end to the slave stack top. The actual linked end includes another 212 bytes, leaving 105,260 bytes to that top and 109,356 bytes to the HWRAM end. Master SP starts at 0x06004000 and grows downward, per SDK crt0. I reran both static layout/budget verifiers; both pass. The parent accepted corrected terminology. Candidate-1 per-VBlank register samples observed master SP as low as 0x0600200c and slave SP as low as 0x060fee8c; these are sampled values, not proven worst-case stack bounds.

## Declined to judge, with reasons

- **Physical Saturn operation, retail BIOS boot, drive compatibility, PAL behavior and exact hardware speed:** no physical-console observation is available. Structural BIN/CUE validation and the NTSC-path HLE capture do not establish these properties.
- **Hardware bus contention between VDP1 rendering/DMA and the slave music module in the VRAM tail:** source ranges are disjoint and scheduling was inspected, but software-rendered Yabause is not a hardware bus measurement.
- **Master/slave worst-case stack depth and heap/stack separation under every path:** static placement passes and sampled registers are available; no full execution high-water instrumentation or whole-program stack bound was run.
- **All human input routes, every unsupported gameplay dependency and all tournaments/modes:** this deliverable is a direct TAG fixture. Targeted ROM cases and recorded legal matches do not exhaust the native runtime's input/state space.
- **Unconditional zero CD reads or full speed after arbitrary human takeover:** resident atlas/page selection is trace based. Cold fallback remains necessary; the final atlas gives150 cold slots, but that is not an all-input residency proof.
- **Full game ROM equivalence:** the independent oracle proves the targeted branches and saved phase cases. It does not execute every original owner/state transition in every reachable context. Condition codes and address-register side effects are outside tests that explicitly compare RAM/RNG and D0–D7.
- **Exact original PCB refresh frequency:** the project uses MAME's documented emulation timing reference. No physical PCB timing was measured here.
- **Exact arcade audio waveform, instrument balance, music transcription or physical Saturn analog output:** native firmware state, DSP cadence and actual audible callback samples are checked; no calibrated original-audio comparison was performed.
- **Full-scene pixel identity at every game tick:** tile crop/raster equivalence is strong and source-based; complete original-versus-native video alignment and palette/layer equality for every scene was not audited in this pass.
- **Future performance changes and any later executable/atlas:** this pass pins candidate2's exact hashes. New profiling or optimization work is not approved by reuse of candidate2's evidence.

## Assessment

Candidate2 is a concrete, independently verifiable native TAG demonstration with a genuine emulator recording, native audio, live-controller takeover and a structurally valid raw disc suitable for a physical trial. The prior configuration, direction, state6 and replaced-WAV findings have concrete closures. No new critical implementation defect was identified in the inspected crop, CD transport, replay adapter or generic emulator corrections. **It does not yet satisfy an unqualified minimum-arcade-speed requirement, and this core cannot establish physical Saturn raster throughput.** A delivery of candidate2 must state those limits; any further performance candidate requires fresh measurement and a focused review of its changes.
